## Confirmed correct (no change needed)

- 22K = 91.6% (916), 18K = 75% (750), 14K = 58.5% (585), 20K = 833, 23K = 958 ([BIS FAQ](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/hallmarking-faqs/hallmarking-faq))
- HUID is a **6-character alphanumeric** code, checkable with "Verify HUID" in the **BIS Care** app ([BIS consumer protection](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/consumer-protection/))
- Mandatory hallmarking began in **June 2021** in 256 districts ([Punekar News](https://www.punekarnews.in/hallmarking-of-gold-made-mandatory-in-256-districts-from-today/))
- GST on gold and silver jewellery, coins and bars is **3%**; CGST 1.5% + SGST 1.5% within a state ([GST Council FAQ](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/sectoral-fq-gems-jewellery.pdf))
- Sellers may not accept **₹2 lakh or more in cash** for one purchase ([IIFL on Section 269ST](https://www.iifl.com/index.php/blogs/gold-loan/gold-cash-purchase-limit-india))
- Unhallmarked old jewellery can be tested at BIS centres and you get a report (BIS consumer protection, above)
- Units: 1 pavan = 8 g, 1 tola = 11.6638 g, 1 troy ounce = 31.1035 g, 1 diamond carat = 0.2 g (standard definitions)
- Gold ETFs and gold mutual funds are SEBI-regulated (SEBI advisory coverage, above)

- **Silver hallmarking** is voluntary today but may become mandatory. Re-check before the next update.
